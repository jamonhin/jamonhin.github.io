## 2. Title formula

```
[What the viewer gets] | Confident with AI #[n]
```

Lead with the benefit or the question, not the module number. "Module 2,
Lesson 3" tells a searcher nothing. "What never goes into an AI prompt" does.

Keep under 60 characters before the pipe so it doesn't truncate on mobile.

## 6. Practical settings

| Setting | Use |
|---|---|
| Visibility | Public |
| Category | Education |
| Audience | **Not made for kids** — getting this wrong disables comments |
| Language | English |
| Playlist | Confident with AI |
| Comments | On — you want the questions |

**Thumbnails.** Make one template and change the text per video. Big readable
words (4–5 max), high contrast, consistent colour so the series looks like a
series. You already have `assets/img/training/Course-0.png` through `Course-3.png`
as a starting point.

**Subtitles.** YouTube auto-generates them. Skim the first one — auto-captions
mangle technical terms, and this audience includes people watching with sound
off at work.

**End screens.** Last 20 seconds: link to the next video in the series plus
the course page.

**Upload order.** All 25 at once as unlisted, check them, then switch to public
in order. Avoids publishing #12 before #11 by accident.
